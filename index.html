Yaris Garage

Four ways to play with the 2010 Toyota Yaris crash-test model, drawn straight with WebGL.

  • Explode

    Pull the car apart: 926 parts, tap one to see what it is.

  • Drive

    Cruise an endless road through hills, lakes and forest.

  • Rally

    Night gravel stage in the rain. Jumps, slides, headlights.

  • Puzzle

    Rebuild the car part by part, inner layers first.

Car geometry: 2010 Toyota Yaris finite element model, Center for Collision Safety and Analysis (CCSA), George Mason University, sponsored by FHWA / NHTSA.
